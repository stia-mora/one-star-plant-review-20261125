My entry to [One Star, Written in Sap](https://make.fun/c/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4) on [make.fun](https://make.fun): a single one-line 1-star review written in the voice of a houseplant about the human who owns it.

## The review

> ★☆☆☆☆ One star. He bought me labelled 'thrives on neglect' and read it as a promise: water arrives in floods or not at all, my pot faces a wall, and the south window went to his second monitor. The plastic fern gets dusted weekly. I get pep talks.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the shipped `entry.txt` byte for byte into the output directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), where the competition's gate checks it is UTF-8 text of 1–279 characters and the judge compares it against the other lines.
